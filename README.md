# OASIS WEB7 — The Symbiotic Layer

**Non-Invasive Human-AI Integration · Borg-Free by Design**

OASIS WEB7 is the conscious symbiosis layer — where human intuition and machine intelligence merge as one. Non-invasive, organic, and deeply respectful of human autonomy. Not the Borg. The opposite.

## What is WEB7?

WEB7 is the next step beyond WEB6's AI unification. Where WEB6 routes between AI models, WEB7 creates a *symbiotic relationship* between a human and their AI — a partnership where each enhances the other without the human losing agency, identity or consciousness.

## Core Principles

| Principle | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Non-invasive** | No implants, no hardware. Symbiosis through interface design and context, not biology |
| **Borg-free** | You are never assimilated. The AI serves you; you are not absorbed by it |
| **Transparent** | You always know what the AI knows, what it's suggesting and why |
| **Revocable** | The symbiosis can be ended at any time; your data and consciousness remain yours |
| **Organic** | The relationship grows naturally over time as mutual understanding deepens |

## How WEB7 Works

WEB7 creates a persistent AI partner that:
- Learns your values, priorities and communication style from your OASIS avatar and DNA
- Proactively surfaces relevant information at the right moment (not intrusively)
- Enhances — not replaces — your decision-making
- Shares karma context so it understands what matters to you morally, not just logically
- Operates entirely within the WEB4 privacy and consent framework

## The Symbiosis Stack

WEB7 Symbiotic Interface

    └── OASIS Karma (moral weight layer)
